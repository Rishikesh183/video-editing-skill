# Style: cutout-news (from the creator's reference "edit1", analysed frame by frame)

- Layout mix: mostly full-screen creator; player cut-outs (background removed) rise from the bottom edge and
  stand at the bottom-left/right beside him whenever a player is named, 1 then 2 side by side.
- News moments: a stack of 1-2 photo cards with a coloured headline plate ("REPORTS" red/yellow) zoom in
  over a blurred stadium; framed photos sit over a dark bokeh background with a slow push.
- Statistic screenshots (scorecards, results) shown full screen; a reaction image for humour is allowed.
- Transitions: white flash into a reveal, a red light-leak flash back to the face; whoosh/pop on every
  cut-out and card - something new every 1-2 s.
- Building blocks: player-cutout (most used), news-cards, photo-full with flash, photo-frames duel for
  comparisons, stat-hero for a big number.
- Do: put a face on screen whenever a player is named. Don't: leave the creator alone for more than 4 s.
