# Creator's standing preferences

Learned from the creator's feedback on earlier edits. They override general editing habits. New lessons
are appended at the bottom when the creator ticks "Remember this" on a re-plan note.

## Look
- No dark text on orange, and never an outline on text that sits on a coloured plate. Dark text only on
  yellow or white plates; white text with a black outline on footage; yellow for the payoff word.
- Never a plain dark-blue box with text, and do not blur the creator's own shot behind text (at most once,
  only if nothing else fits). Text moments get a designed colour stage plus a related picture.
- Do not use the cricket-pitch background.
- Bold, modern type (Archivo Black / Montserrat / Inter / Barlow Condensed), never thin or plain.
- Spacing must be clean: nothing stacked on top of something else, no text over the creator's face.

## Pictures
- Every text or number moment needs a RELATED picture: the moment photo, or the players' cut-outs at
  the bottom. "Sri Lanka chased 430" -> the Sri Lanka win photo; "batting depth" -> the two batters' cut-outs.
- Two-sided comparisons (then vs now, player vs player) are a DUEL: one photo from the top, one from the
  bottom, red and blue frames, VS between. Never two photos piled on each other.
- A player slot is a solo photo of exactly that player; the two sides always use different people.

## Words and numbers
- Show the idea, not the words. "100% neck to neck" is a figure of speech: show the two sides with the
  real numbers that make it close, never a lone "100%".
- Never invent numbers (no "1-1" unless it was said).
- Text never wraps unexpectedly: short lines, payoff 1-3 words.

## Timing and motion
- A visual about a person starts when he is NAMED and stays while he is discussed, not 2 s early.
- Smooth, eye-catching motion; nothing snaps. Every entrance has a matching sound, but no sound effects
  in the first 3.5 s (the hook is the creator's voice).
- When the creator is cut out and shrunk, he sits in the bottom-left corner with no gap below.

## Re-plans
- A re-plan UPDATES the current plan (fix what is wrong, keep what works); it does not start over.
