# Creator's standing preferences for voice videos

Learned from the creator's feedback. They override general habits. New lessons are appended when the creator
ticks "Remember this" on a re-plan note.

## Look
- The reference is the Physics Witness demo: dark asphalt ground, one strong accent colour, an investigation
  file feel, stacked cards and folders, chips, diagrams that build up, bold condensed headlines.
- Colours change from video to video to match the script. The structure of the look stays.
- Clean spacing: nothing stacked on top of something else, and text never wraps unexpectedly.

## Sound
- No whooshes, swooshes or whips. Use clicks, soft clicks, taps, ticks, pops and key sounds.
- Sound effects are sparse: too many are disturbing. Mark only the arrivals that matter, not every element.

## Words and numbers
- Show the idea, not the words. Never invent numbers, results or quotes.

## Re-plans
- A re-plan UPDATES the current plan (fix what is wrong, keep what works); it does not start over.
